title: A new site for EmiBSD
description: www.emibsd.org now explains the project, the process and its status in a few pages.
---
The single landing page that used to live at emibsd.org is now a small site:

- [About]({{ '/about/' | relative_url }}): why EmiBSD exists and how it relates to OpenBSD.
- [Process]({{ '/process/' | relative_url }}): from the 1:1 port in EmiBSD.LZ to the redesign
  here, and the agents that do the work.
- [Status]({{ '/status/' | relative_url }}): every kernel subsystem, moved, in progress or
  pending.
- [Docs]({{ '/docs/' | relative_url }}): building, booting in QEMU and the layout of the tree.
- [Contributing]({{ '/contributing/' | relative_url }}): issues, target hardware and a FAQ.

Where things stand today: milestones N0 to N3 are met. Memory management (uvm) now lives in the
`kernel` crate, which the compiler refuses to build with any `unsafe`. N4, processes and
scheduling, is under way: timeouts, processes and credentials, file descriptors, kqueue,
select(2) and poll(2) have moved; the scheduler is next. 44 of the 331 entries of the
system-call table are now handled natively by the `abi` module.

EmiBSD is pre-alpha and runs only in QEMU. There is no release yet.
